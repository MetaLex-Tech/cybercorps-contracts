# Formation packets and signing

The company's Documents workspace prepares a post-formation packet from
its saved formation details. Review the company, officers, directors,
founders, capitalization and proposed ownership before preparing documents.
A staged founder position remains a draft until issuance is recorded.

## Review the packet's documents and roles

The packet can include bylaws, an initial Board consent, founder purchase
and invention-assignment documents, and notices of issuance. A company
filed through the formation service uses the service-filed variant: it
omits an Action of Incorporator and accounts for the incorporator's earlier
resolutions. Confirm the formation history instead of selecting documents
only by their names.

Each document names its signing roles and capacities. Link a founder to
the correct staged position, then review the holder, shares, price and
vesting. The packet owner sees who must act on each document and can follow
links between the packet and **Securities status**.

## Save, approve and prepare

Saving creates a packet revision. Review the rendered PDFs and resolve
missing information and signing authority before approving preparation.
The app identifies changes that replace open invitations. Signed documents
remain tied to the revisions their signers reviewed; a later draft does not
rewrite that evidence.

Preparation checks the company's actual component versions and the target
registry. Eligible requests use creator-bound agreement creation, committing
the complete initial terms before signatures are exposed. Existing requests
keep their earlier registration strategy. Registration, signature collection,
recording and chain finality remain distinct states.

Where company sponsorship is enabled, the owner approves the stated credit
and gas limits before background preparation and recording. A blocked
preparation shows what still needs attention; retry after resolving it.

## Invite each person and collect their signatures

The app groups a recipient's packet invitations so they can accept them
with one wallet and work through prepared documents in one sitting. The
recipient still reviews and signs each document in its assigned capacity.
A link or email acceptance alone does not execute the packet.

Use the invitation and delivery status to check whether MetaLeX sent the
message or whether you need to copy a link. The recipient must authenticate
as the invited account. Wrong-account and wallet mismatches must be resolved
before signing. Completion notices and delivery retries depend on the
configured service and the request's status.

## Connect the packet to issuance

A finalized packet Board consent can satisfy the approval gate for the
specific Schedule 3 position it covers. The app compares the holder, class,
shares, price and, for supported restricted stock, the complete vesting
terms. A mismatch needs correction; it is not an instruction to issue a
different position.

**Securities status** distinguishes an approval linked before issuance from
one recorded afterward. An after-issuance link preserves the issue date and
does not retroactively represent that approval was checked before issuance.
An officer can make an explicit decision to proceed without a linked Board
consent where the flow allows it; the app records that decision.

For eligible plain founder purchases, the owner supplies the issue date
and authorizes recording after the required launch documents have finalized.
The Notice of Issuance can then follow the recorded issuance. Restricted
purchases retain their applicable document requirements. Review the
requirements displayed for the actual position rather than assuming that
all packet documents gate every issuance in the same way.

## Download the closeout

The closeout ZIP uses each document's signed revision. It includes the
available executed evidence, conformed copies, completion certificate and
a README for counsel. A generated conformed copy does not replace the
original signed PDF. Check unresolved signing or recording states before
relying on the packet as complete.

The packet coordinates documents and application records. Creating it does
not itself form a legal entity, issue a LET or grant an officer authority.
See [Form a company](formation.md), [The cap table](captable.md) and
[cyberSign](cybersign.md) for those separate steps.
